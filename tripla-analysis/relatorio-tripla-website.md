## 1. Solutions offered

Tripla organizes its entire portfolio under three strategic pillars — **Disponibilidade (Availability)**, **Proteção (Protection)**, and **Conformidade (Compliance)** — delivered by three dedicated internal teams (Tech Operations, Cyber Operations, GRC Operations) that the company says work in an integrated cycle rather than as silos ("O Método Tripla": Diagnóstico → Arquitetura → Implantação → Integração → Sustentação).

### Proteção (Cybersecurity)
- **SOC / MDR 24x7 — CyberWatch** (proprietary platform, AI-assisted detection and response)
- **SASE / ZTNA** (Zero Trust Network Access)
- **WAF / API Shield** (web application & API protection)
- **EDR** (endpoint detection & response)
- **Microssegmentação** (network microsegmentation)
- **AI Security** — security for AI agents (dedicated guide/playbook)
- **Human Risk Management** — security awareness, phishing simulation, LGPD/ISO 27001 awareness training
- **Insider Risk** — behavioral/user-activity monitoring
- **Proteção OT** — operational technology / industrial security
- **Proteção de Identidade** — PAM and IAM
- **Gestão de Vulnerabilidades** — VM and CTEM (continuous threat exposure management)
- **Firewall / SD-WAN Enterprise**
- **Videomonitoramento / video analytics**

### Disponibilidade (Infrastructure & Continuity)
- Corporate connectivity / networking (FTTO-GPON, Wi-Fi 6/7)
- Firewall / SD-WAN
- **BESS** — battery energy storage systems
- Data Center / Colocation
- **Data Recovery** (backup & disaster recovery)
- **Trisky** — proprietary secure VDI / remote-work governance SaaS
- **PCN** — business continuity planning

### Conformidade (Governance, Risk & Compliance)
- Data & access governance (notably a dedicated **Varonis → Netwrix migration** offer)
- **DPO as a Service**
- **TPRM** — third-party risk management
- **vCISO**
- **Governança algorítmica** — AI governance (referencing ISO 42001 and the EU AI Act)
- **Autoritas** — proprietary 360° GRC platform (compliance, risk, third parties, audit)
- LGPD / ISO 27001 conformity & awareness programs

### Proprietary products (the three "Produtos by Tripla")
| Product | Category | Public URL |
|---|---|---|
| **CyberWatch** | SOC/MDR platform | cyberwatch.com.br |
| **Trisky** | VDI / remote-work governance SaaS | trisky.ia.br |
| **Autoritas** | GRC 360° platform | autoritas.com.br |

Owning IP in all three pillars (not just reselling) is the clearest structural differentiator versus a pure systems-integrator model.

## 4. Critical assessment

### Strengths
- **Full-stack, three-pillar integrator model** (infrastructure + cybersecurity + GRC) rather than a point-solution vendor — lets Tripla sell one relationship across a client's entire tech/risk stack.
- **Proprietary IP in all three pillars** (CyberWatch, Trisky, Autoritas) — higher margin, stickier accounts, and a story that doesn't reduce to "we resell Cloudflare/Okta/Qualys."
- **Strong, well-known global manufacturer bench** (Cloudflare, Okta, Qualys, BeyondTrust, Trend Micro, Huawei, Check Point, Illumio, TXOne, Cohesity, Netwrix) spanning nearly every enterprise security/infra category.
- **Credible technical bench strength**: ISO 27001/22301/20000-1, IAPP privacy credentials, EC-Council, HCIE — unusually broad certification spread for a mid-size Brazilian integrator, and useful in RFPs that score team credentials.
- **Brazil-native, 24x7 delivery** (NOC/SOC "em território brasileiro") — a real differentiator against offshore-delivered MSSPs on language, timezone, and LGPD data-residency comfort.
- **Diversified, blue-chip vertical proof points**: iFood, Volkswagen, SIMPAR, multiple Unimed federations, Hermes Pardini/Fleury, State Grid — spans retail/marketplace, financial services, healthcare, energy, logistics, industry and BPO/call center.
- **Active thought-leadership/PR presence** (IT Section, Information Management quoting Roberto Toscani on AI governance) — positions Tripla's leadership as a market voice, useful top-of-funnel signal for the sales team.

### Weaknesses / risks
- **Structural dependence on third-party manufacturers.** Most of the portfolio value still sits on Cloudflare/Okta/Qualys/etc. licensing; a manufacturer moving to direct sales, or a competing integrator winning a program, is a real margin and renewal risk that owning three internal products only partly offsets.
- **Visible partner churn signal.** A dedicated "Varonis → Netwrix migration" page implies Tripla shifted a manufacturer relationship (or is capturing clients migrating away from Varonis generally) — worth confirming internally so sales doesn't get caught off guard if a prospect asks about the Varonis history.
- **Heavy use of anonymized case studies** ("cliente sigiloso do segmento siderurgia," "grupo industrial multinacional," "multinacional brasileira líder em call center") — understandable under NDA, but it measurably weakens the proof-point punch for prospects who want name-brand references, especially in industrial/DR and DDoS-response stories that would otherwise be strong.
- **Portfolio breadth risk.** 25+ named solution lines spanning cybersecurity, GRC, VDI, video surveillance, and even battery energy storage (BESS) creates a "jack of all trades" perception risk and a harder positioning story — BESS and video/CCTV in particular read as adjacent infra plays rather than core cyber/GRC, and could dilute the "cybersecurity enterprise" headline the homepage leads with.
- **No visible pricing/packaging tiers** — expected for enterprise B2B, but it also means every deal is a bespoke sales cycle with no anchor for smaller/faster-moving prospects.
- **Domestic-only footprint.** Everything on the site is Brazil-specific; there's no visible LatAm or global-account story, which may cap appeal to multinational HQs evaluating a single global MSSP.

### Market positioning
Tripla positions itself as a **Brazilian "high level" systems integrator / MSSP / GRC-as-a-service provider** for enterprise and upper-mid-market accounts in regulated, operationally-critical verticals (healthcare, financial services, energy, industry, logistics, retail, call center/BPO). Its pitch is consolidation: one relationship spanning infrastructure availability, cybersecurity, and compliance, wrapped in three proprietary products and backed by a large certification bench. Its natural competitive set is Brazilian systems integrators/MSSPs (e.g., regional MSSPs and larger names like Stefanini/TIVIT-style players) and boutique LGPD/GRC consultancies, plus global MSSPs expanding into Brazil. Owning CyberWatch (SOC/MDR), Trisky (VDI) and Autoritas (GRC) is the thing a pure reseller can't easily copy, and is the most defensible long-term asset in the story.

### Overall impression
A credibly-scaled (+130 clients, 10+ years, 70% YoY growth), certification-heavy Brazilian integrator with a coherent three-pillar narrative and real proprietary IP — stronger on breadth and delivery credibility than on crisp positioning. The biggest near-term opportunity is sharpening the story (the BESS/video lines dilute an otherwise strong "cybersecurity + compliance" narrative) and converting more of the anonymized case studies into named references as NDAs allow.
